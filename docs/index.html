01 — What it does

Built for actually watching anime

Not a wrapper around a website. A real Compose app with a real player, on every screen you own.

Twenty-plus sources

Streams resolve across Miruro, Senshi, AniBD, AniKoto, KickAssAnime, AllAnime, AnimeKai and more, with automatic fallback when one is missing an episode.

Filter to the track you want

Narrow the server list by sub or dub, then by spoken language. A Hindi dub or a French sub is a tap away instead of a hunt through every server.

Offline downloads

Save episodes with their subtitle tracks and watch them with no connection. Progress, storage use and removal all live in the Library.

A real 10-foot TV app

A purpose-built leanback layout for Android TV and Fire TV, fully navigable by remote — down to Fire OS 5 sticks on API 22.

Resume exactly where you left

Continue watching keeps precise positions per episode, on device, whether or not you ever sign in to anything.

AniList & MyAnimeList sync

Bring your watchlist, scores and progress along. Anilili writes episode progress back as you watch — or stays entirely local if you prefer.

Subtitles that behave

Size, colour, background opacity and vertical position are all yours, plus a delay control for when a track drifts out of sync.

Skip intro & auto-advance

Intro and outro markers come from AniSkip, so you can jump the opening and roll straight into the next episode without touching anything.

Rank your own servers

Pin up to three preferred servers in order. The in-player picker still switches source for a single episode without disturbing that.

Browse the whole catalogue

Search and filter by genre, tag, year, format, status and rating, with a release schedule so you know what airs when.

Cast to the big screen

Chromecast support for the sources that allow it, with the same controls you get in the app.

Free, always

No ads, no accounts required, no paywall and no telemetry. Open source on GitHub and distributed as a signed APK.

Sideloading, briefly

  1. Take the universal APK if you are unsure which you need — it runs everywhere.
  2. Allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks. Once only.
  3. Open the file and install. On a TV stick, use Downloader or send it over from your phone.
  4. Anilili checks for its own updates from then on, so this is the last time you need this page.
